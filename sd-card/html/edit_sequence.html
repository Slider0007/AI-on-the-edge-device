💠Number Sequences
CLICK HERE for usage info

Define your number sequences by grouping digit and analog ROIs (Region Of Interest) for processing. Use the dropdown to manage sequences or create new ones; a main sequence is provided by default. You can toggle between ROI types and individual regions using the selection boxes, or click New to automatically generate a new ROI.

Position ROIs by dragging them with your mouse or manually entering coordinates. Their order determines their multiplication factor in the final reading—use the Move Lower and Move Higher buttons to adjust this sequence. The resulting decimal scaling is displayed below the selection boxes for verification.
Once finished, click Save And Apply to activate your changes immediately without a reboot.

For detailed placement rules, refer to the ROI Configuration Documentation.
